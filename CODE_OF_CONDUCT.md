# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not people. Assume the other person is smart and acting in good faith
until they prove otherwise.

## Expected

- Technical disagreement, stated directly, with reasoning.
- "This is wrong because X" is good. "This is wrong because you're an idiot" is not.
- Accepting that a maintainer may say no, and that a contributor may say "I still think you're wrong".
- Reporting security issues privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Deliberately wasting maintainer time: spam PRs, AI-generated slop opened at volume, or
  "contributions" that are marketing.
- Using this project's issues or discussions to promote a token.

## Crypto-specific

This is a wallet, which attracts a specific kind of bad behaviour:

- **No shilling.** Do not open issues, PRs, or discussions to promote an unrelated token or service.
- **No price talk.** This is a tool, not a trading floor.
- **Never ask anyone for a seed phrase, passphrase, or private key**, for any reason, including
  "debugging". No maintainer will ever ask you for one. Anyone who does is attacking you.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues via a GitHub issue for public matters, or a private security advisory for
anything sensitive. Maintainers may remove comments, close PRs, or ban accounts. There is no
appeals process; this is a small project, not a court.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, its issues and PRs, and anywhere someone is representing the project.
